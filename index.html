Welcome to the Ceramic Club!

Everyone of all levels are welcome to join us during open hours for wheel throwing, hand building, trimming, glazing, and everything in between. However, due to the high numbers of interest we'll be posting a signup sheet for a guaranteed time on the wheel. With that being said, the last hour of every open hour will be unreserved wheel slots which are walk-ins friendly. The sign up sheet only pertains to the wheel; if you want to hand build, trim, glaze, or anything else clay related, you are welcome to walk-in anytime during open hours (no signup needed). Find the Ceramics Club on the first floor of Mark Hopkins, near the Purple Bike workshops.
